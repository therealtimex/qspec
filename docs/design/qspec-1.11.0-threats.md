# qspec 1.11.0: threats to identification, named and answered

Design brief from System Design, 2026-09-06. Delivery target: a branch `qspec-1.11.0` on `/Users/realtimex/github/qspec` (GitHub `therealtimex/qspec`) from `main` at 25e5ad7 (v1.10.2). No forge, as for 1.7.0 to 1.10.2: delivery is the pushed branch at a named SHA; do not tag or release, System Design does that after QA.

## Context

In empirical economics the identification strategy is the question's viability: a proposal that does not name its endogeneity threats and say how the design answers them is not accepted. The causal profile in the social overlay holds `identifying_assumption`, `main_alternative_explanation`, `design_risk`, and `precommitted_checks`: one assumption, one alternative, one risk. On the six vn-procurement specs the reviewers raised non-random inspection targeting, a pipeline confound, and manipulation at a statutory threshold, and each landed in whichever of those fields fit. A committee reading the sheet sees three sentences in three places and has to reconstruct the argument. The research brief that started this project says it in its own words: consider endogeneity, selection, reverse causality, omitted variables, measurement error, alternative explanations, and violations of identifying assumptions.

## Decisions

- **ADR-1.11.0-1 A `design` field on the causal profile, from a catalog.** `profile.design` names the identification design: `regression_discontinuity`, `difference_in_differences`, `event_study`, `instrumental_variables`, `matching`, `randomized_experiment`, `synthetic_control`, `bunching`, `other`. Optional in 1.x. The catalog lives in `schema/catalogs.json` under the social domain with a label and a sentence form for each value, and the overlay's section 4.1 lists them.
- **ADR-1.11.0-2 A `threats_to_identification` list on the causal profile.** Each entry has `threat` (a catalog value), `why_it_applies` (this setting, not a textbook definition), and `response` (what the design or a check does about it). The generic threat catalog is the brief's own list: `selection`, `reverse_causality`, `omitted_variables`, `measurement_error`, `simultaneity`, `spillovers`, `attrition`, `anticipation`. Each design adds its standard threats in the catalog: for regression discontinuity `manipulation_of_running_variable` and `bandwidth_sensitivity`; for difference-in-differences and event studies `parallel_trends`, `staggered_timing`, `anticipation`; for instrumental variables `exclusion_restriction`, `weak_instruments`, `monotonicity`; for matching `selection_on_unobservables`, `common_support`; for randomised experiments `compliance`, `attrition`, `spillovers`; for synthetic control `donor_pool_validity`, `pre_period_fit`; for bunching `density_manipulation`, `counterfactual_density`. Optional in 1.x; the templates carry one empty entry with a comment naming the catalog.
- **ADR-1.11.0-3 Two warnings, no block, no M invariant.** `threats-unaddressed`: the spec names a design and one or more of that design's standard threats do not appear in the list; the message names them. `threat-without-check`: an entry's `response` names no item of `precommitted_checks` and no clause of `kill_condition`; "names" means a case-insensitive substring match of at least four consecutive words, or an explicit `check:` key on the entry pointing at a `precommitted_checks` item by index or text. A response that is not a runnable check is reassurance, which is what the second warning is for. Both fire only for `method_family: empirical_causal` and only when the list or the design is present, so every 1.10.x spec lints as before. Core section 8.4 lists both; section 14 records that a required field on the profile is deferred to 2.0.
- **ADR-1.11.0-4 The reviewer's rule mentions it.** The overlay's J7 rule for the causal profile stays as it is; a sentence under it in section 4.1 says the reviewer reads each threat's response against the check it names and signs nothing that answers a threat with a claim. `sign --show` prints the profile's J7 as before; the SKILL.md interview step asks the writer for the design and its threats by name.
- **ADR-1.11.0-5 The sheet renders the table a committee reads.** Under Design, after the labelled fields, a section "Threats to identification and how the design answers them" as a table with columns Threat, Why it applies here, Response, using labels from the catalog, and the design named in the design sentence ("The design is a regression discontinuity…"). The dossier renders the same table. `committee-clean` covers the new labels through the label completeness check. When the list is empty the section is omitted; a `--draft` sheet lists "Threats to identification" under "Before submission" when the design is named and the list is empty.
- **ADR-1.11.0-6 Overlays for other domains.** The natural-science and engineering overlays gain the same structure as `threats_to_validity` with their own catalogs, small for now: natural `confounding`, `instrument_drift`, `sampling`, `selection`, `measurement_error`; engineering `baseline_fairness`, `workload_validity`, `measurement_error`, `configuration_drift`, `benchmark_overfitting`. Same two warnings, same rendering. Document them in sections 4 of QSPEC-NS.md and QSPEC-ENG.md; keep the design catalog social-only until a real spec in those domains needs one.
- **ADR-1.11.0-7 Versioning and docs.** Minor release 1.11.0: optional fields, catalog entries, two warnings, rendering. Every 1.10.2 spec, record, and Index is a valid 1.11.0 one. Bump every version string the bundle check holds and the four schema document headers; core sections 7, 8.4, 14, 15; overlay sections 4.1 and the NS and ENG equivalents; `scripts/check-judged.mjs` or the label check covers the new catalogs; CHANGELOG, README, SKILL.md follow the earlier entries. The guidance fingerprint is unchanged unless the block gains text; if it does, `init --refresh` clears STALE as before.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `npm test` passes, with new checks: a causal spec naming `design: regression_discontinuity` with no threats listed reports `threats-unaddressed` naming `manipulation_of_running_variable` and `bandwidth_sensitivity`; a spec whose response matches no pre-committed check or kill clause reports `threat-without-check` and one whose response quotes a check does not; the example causal spec gains a design and three threats and lints clean; the sheet for it contains the threats table with catalog labels and passes `committee-clean`; a `--draft` sheet for a design with no threats lists the item under "Before submission"; a 1.10.2 spec without the fields produces no new finding; the label completeness check covers every new catalog value.
2. `node scripts/plugin.mjs --check` passes and `scripts/check-judged.mjs` still holds the overlays and the catalog to the same J7 text.
3. Proof, reported in the Dev handoff with the run name: a temp project from `examples/`, `render --out documents`, `paperforge all --draft` with the deployed paperforge 4.0.1; the Q-101 sheet html and pdf show the threats table and verify `ok`.
4. Nothing under `policyforge/vietnam/vn-procurement` is modified. Backfilling designs and threats on Q-001 to Q-006 is the writer's work in a later loop, alongside their citation keys.

## Non-goals

- No required field; that is a 2.0 change.
- No tool judgment of whether a response is credible; the reviewer signs that.
- No change to the Decision Record, the Index, or paperforge.
